5 · Insights

Two or more risk factors mean 2.7× the early attrition of the no-factor group

Risk factors: pay ratio below 0.90, onboarding score below 3.0, overtime of 8+ h/week, manager change | thresholds are exploratory

44%

16% of hires carry two or more risk factors but account for 31% of exits; their rate is 2.7× the no-factor group.

78%

78% of exits are voluntary, and up to 72% of recorded reasons sit within the organisation's influence.

These are associations, not causes. Thresholds were chosen on this data and should be validated on a new cohort.
